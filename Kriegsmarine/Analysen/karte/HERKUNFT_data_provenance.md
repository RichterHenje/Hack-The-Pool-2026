# 21 · Data provenance of the ArcGIS demo map

Generated by `scripts/arcgis_provenance.py` from `scripts/arcgis/ke_sources.json`; counts from the live layers (2026-10-28T10:55:57.473Z), team data at commit a4b8b5a. Do not edit by hand.

## Status words

- **checked by a person**: a named check by a team member exists
- **machine-made, unchecked**: produced by a script, OCR or a match; a proposal until a person checks it
- **rule-based**: follows a fixed, written rule (e.g. arrival date = end of the leg); can be wrong where the rule does not fit

## Stations (`KE1893_stops`)

48 stations of the 1892–93 voyage; 47 with an arrival date.

**Status:** rule-based (dates), machine-made (Kulturpool counts).

How it was made:

- Stations and their order: the team's vault, matched to Höfer's route overview.
- Arrival date = end of the leg into the place in Höfer's route overview (not the first mention). 5 dates are estimated.
- Coordinates and today's names: GeoNames via the team's gazetteer.
- Kulturpool count: records that name the place and are dated 1890–1895 (voyage years ±2); 16 of 48 stations have at least one. The count is about the place, not the voyage.
- Corrected on 28 October 2026: two stations had wrong GeoNames matches (Livingston in New Jersey, Wyoming in Michigan) and now sit at Livingston, Montana (the team's hand-set point of the diary day 20.9.1893) and Yellowstone National Park (Wikidata Q351); Tjiandjur's arrival follows Höfer (15.4.1893) instead of an estimate (6.5.1893).

Checks:

- Höfer's route against the diary: 143 of 170 diary days name the same place by string, 14 differ (spelling or OCR, read by an AI agent; not checked by a person).

Sources: Höfer (ed.) 2010; Franz Ferdinand, Tagebuch 1895 (Internet Archive); KHM team, Hack the Pool 2026; GeoNames; Kulturpool.

Data: team data commit b5e1464; inputs unchanged at a4b8b5a; corrections of 28.10. (see above).

## Diary, day by day (`KE1893_days`)

216 of the diary's 309 days have a place and are on the map.

**Status:** machine-made, unchecked (dates partly rule-based); coordinate origin given per day.

How it was made:

- Day date: 84 days have an entry of their own, 129 are part of a multi-day entry and were assigned by matching the team's English day page, 3 have no entry of their own. OCR lost many headings, so an assigned date can be 1–5 days early.
- Coordinate basis per day (team field coord_source): Wikidata 98, GeoNames 63, estimated 15, set by hand (approximate) 40; 169 days link their Wikidata or GeoNames record in the pop-up ('Beleg').
- Coordinate method: hunting place 87, camp or station of a hunting place 28, place in the day's heading 37, same place on another day 17, hand list 41, route place 6.
- Every day links to its page scan; quote from the scan, not from the OCR.

Checks:

- Team commit 61a4de9 (28 October 2026) checked the day coordinates and added their origin; who checked is not recorded in the data, so this map counts it as not checked by a person.

Sources: Franz Ferdinand, Tagebuch 1895 (Internet Archive); KHM team, Hack the Pool 2026; Wikidata; GeoNames.

Data: team data commit a4b8b5a (28.10.).

## Photographs, Weltmuseum Wien (`KE1893_photos_all`)

357 of the team's 408 photographs are placed (128 with a day date, shown with the time slider; the rest in the layer 'ohne Tagesdatum'). 51 name only a region and are not on the map.

**Status:** rule-based (place), licence per record.

How it was made:

- Point = the place the museum or the team names, not Franz Ferdinand's position.
- Place tiers: A 236 team gazetteer (route place), A2 6 same via the museum's geography, B 96 Wikidata, T 19 Nepal Terai hunt camp (approximate).
- Licence: 109 photos (108 Europeana records) carry CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 with attribution in the caption; 248 read 'Lizenz nicht geprüft' and are shown with that label (owner decision 28 October 2026; rights not cleared).
- Photos whose museum geography is 'Livingston' had been placed in Livingston, New Jersey by a GeoNames match; on 28 October they were moved to the team's hand-set Livingston, Montana (diary day 20.9.1893), after the team removed the wrong GeoNames entry.

Checks:

- Day-dated photos against the route: 15 of 15 fit by date and country (read by an AI agent; not checked by a person).

Sources: Weltmuseum Wien; Europeana; KHM team, Hack the Pool 2026; Wikidata.

Data: team data commit b5e1464, Livingston corrected after a4b8b5a.
