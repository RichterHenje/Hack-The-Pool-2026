## Script

(slide 1: the day card of 11 January 1893)

Speaker A: On 11 January 1893 the cruiser SMS Kaiserin Elisabeth lay off Ceylon, and the heir to the throne went hunting at Kalawewa. [DEMO 1: reise.html at 1893-01-11] This is one of 309 diary days on the team's page. The ship's archive and Kulturpool describe the same voyage. Where do they meet?

(slide 2: the gap)

Speaker B: We placed 70 of the 90 stations of the ship on a map and asked Kulturpool what it holds for each place. 24 stations have a dated record. Two records name the ship. The museums that hold the voyage are not among the portal's data providers today. The Weltmuseum photographs have been in Europeana since 2019, delivered through Kulturpool, and the portal finds no Tandur-Camp. The delivery exists; the portal does not show it.

(slide 3: against the grain, the ask)

Speaker C: Beside the route we placed the hunting, the animals, and the people in the photographs, day by day, with the source on every card, and we say which sentences are unchecked machine output. The archive stays as it is; the links are a layer beside it. Tell us which records belong to this voyage, connect the museums that hold it, and help us check the hunting sentences against the scans. Thank you.
