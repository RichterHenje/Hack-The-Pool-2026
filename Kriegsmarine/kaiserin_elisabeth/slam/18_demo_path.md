# 18 · Demo path (live, at most 75 seconds)

The live page is the team's `Kriegsmarine/kaiserin_elisabeth/prototype/reise.html` (team repo at
b5e1464; a local copy sits in `reference/team-khm`). Layer toggles on the page: Tage, Jagden,
Tiere, Menschen, Echo (Presse). The interface is German; the speaker names what is on screen in
English. Rehearse the path twice; never type during the slam; the tab is open before the slam
starts. An alternative path on the owner's ArcGIS web map (private, time slider) exists in
`docs/13_arcgis_demo.md`; use it only if the team prefers it.

## Path

1. Before the slam: open `reise.html#1893-01-11` in its own browser tab, full screen, zoomed to the day card; leave it (0 s)
2. [DEMO 1] Day card of 11 January 1893, Kalawewa: the heading, the scan link, the place on the route (15 s)
3. [DEMO 2] Toggle "Jagden" on and point at the hunting sentences on the card; say once that they are unchecked machine output (20 s)
4. [DEMO 3] Open one photograph linked from the card and show the image with its licence line (Weltmuseum Wien, CC BY-NC-SA 4.0): title, inventory number, rights label (20 s)
5. Zoom out to the whole route, "Alle Reisen des Schiffs": the 309 days and the stations (15 s)

Total 70 s. If the page stalls at any step, switch to the fallback at once and keep talking.

## Rights

- Weltmuseum photographs: **decision 28.10. 08:40 (owner): the slam shows the images under the
  Europeana licence CC BY-NC-SA 4.0, with attribution** ("Weltmuseum Wien, via Kulturpool and
  Europeana, CC BY-NC-SA 4.0" beside every image; see `docs/06_slam.md`, claim 5, and
  `docs/04_decisions.md`). The team's earlier rule, links and inventory numbers only
  (`prototype/arcgis/README_photos.md`: "Es gibt nur den Link"), is superseded for the slam; the
  team is told before 14:00. Where the team's page shows only the link, step 4 opens the Europeana
  record of the photograph, which shows the image with its licence.
- Kulturpool records on the page: show only records whose rights statement is open
  (`kp.rights.is_open`) or show the rights label beside the image.
- Newspaper pages (ANNO): link and citation only.
- Diary scans: link to the scan; quotes are read from the scan, not from the OCR text.
