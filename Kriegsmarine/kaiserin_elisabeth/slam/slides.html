Our ask, and the finding behind it

  1. Kulturpool and ACDH
    Trace the 2019 delivery, and tell us which records belong to this voyage.
    The Weltmuseum photographs reached Europeana through Kulturpool in 2019; the portal finds no Tandur-Camp today. 24 of 70 stations have a dated record, 2 records name the ship.
  2. The museums that hold the voyage
    Connect as data providers, or tell us where the records are.
    Weltmuseum Wien, KHM, HGM and Staatsarchiv do not appear as data providers in today's portal.
  3. Everyone who works with sources
    Check the hunting sentences against the scans, with us.
    They are machine-read and nobody has checked them yet. Find us after the slam; we bring the scan links and the blind method we used for the diary days.

Nothing in the archive needs rewriting. It needs connecting.
